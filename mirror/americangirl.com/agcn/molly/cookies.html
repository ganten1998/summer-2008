To play Pedal Power!, you will be using a Shockwave "cookie." This cookie is a small file that is saved on your computer's hard drive. It contains no personal information, like your name or e-mail address, but "remembers" that your computer has visited this site before. So if you leave the site and then return, cookies will be able to remember your high score for each list you help Molly with. Basically, it makes returning to the game more fun!
