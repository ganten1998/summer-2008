To play Count the Miles, you will be using a Shockwave "cookie." This cookie is a small file that is saved on your computer's hard drive. It contains no personal information, like your name or e-mail address, but "remembers" that your computer has visited this site before. So if you leave the site and then return, a cookie will be able to remember the farthest distance you've reached playing the game. Basically, it makes returning to the game more fun!
