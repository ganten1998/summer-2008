Hosting an
American Girl Fashion Show®

The American Girl Fashion Show is an innovative way to raise funds for children’s causes. Featuring historical and contemporary fashions for both girls and their dolls, the show comes alive with your models. A commentator’s script gives the audience girl-friendly pieces of fashion-related history that tie into the stories of the American Girl Characters, as well as insight into how fashion can be a way for girls to express their own unique personalities.

At the show, every child receives fun party favors. Fashion Show guests have the opportunity to purchase event-related souvenirs and have special photographs taken. American Girl books may be available for purchase through a local bookseller, and additional American Girl products are provided as door and raffle prizes.

Audiences come away with terrific memories of their American Girl experience and positive feelings about the mission of the host organization, and about helping others in their community.

To help host organizations succeed, American Girl provides each one with a detailed event manual, key promotional materials, and a personal event consultant to offer expert guidance and advice every step of the way.

Hosting Guidelines
  • Are you affiliated with a nonprofit (501C3) organization?
  • Will the funds you raise benefit children in the United States, ages birth to 12 years?
  • Do you have the capacity to handle attendance of 1,000 to 1,500 guests over the course of a weekend?

If the answers to all of these questions are “yes,” and you would like to learn more about hosting an American Girl Fashion Show®, please call 1-888-325-7953 or e-mail us.
