Glitter Letters

If your best friend has moved away, be sure to send her lots of letters. Use a glitter pen to decorate the envelope, and try writing your friend's name in a fancy kind of writing. You can also put her nickname between her first and last name. It will make her smile.

- Christy, age 11, Idaho
