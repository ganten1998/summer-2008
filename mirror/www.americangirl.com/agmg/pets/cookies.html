To play American Girl magazine's game, Net Pet, you will be using a Flash "cookie." This cookie is a small file that is saved on your computer's hard drive. It contains no personal information, like your name or e-mail address, but "remembers" that your computer has visited this site before. So if you leave the site and then return, cookies will be able to detect your Net Pet's name and characteristics so that you do not have to start all over again. Basically, it makes returning to the game more fun!
