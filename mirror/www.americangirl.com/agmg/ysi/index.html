Super-Sweet Valentines

Here are some unique valentine ideas that put sweet smiles on your friends’ and family’s faces last week. These great ideas can be used anytime you want to let someone know that you care:

I make friendship bracelets for my friends on Valentine’s Day out of pink and red embroidery floss. My friends really like them, and I enjoy making them!
-Molly, age 12, New Jersey

Give a pretty artificial flower as a valentine. Write a sweet message on a brightly colored piece of paper and decorate it with glitter. Attach the cute little card to the flower with ribbon.
-Daera, age 9, Illinois

My idea for a valentine is to make a heart for someone and list all of the reasons why you love that person.
-Brittany, age 10, New York

For my valentines, I bake sugar cookies and frost them with pink, red, or white frosting. Then I write cute messages on them, just like conversation hearts. They are a favorite among my classmates.
-Madilynne, age 12, Connecticut

I never make Valentine's Day cards for my family. Instead, I make coupons. They are really just colored slips of paper that say something like, "Good for one night of doing the dishes," or "Good for one backrub!" Everyone in my family really appreciates them.
-An American girl, age 11, Texas

I make homemade valentines. First, I write “to” and “from” on colored paper. Then I attach a picture of that person and me together. Sometimes I add a little phrase under the picture such as, "Remember this?" or "Great times!" Finally, I trace the letters with glitter.
-Megan, age 10, Pennsylvania

I usually make a dessert for my family as a Valentine’s Day treat. I think it is a unique way to share my talents. I made candy-heart cookies for my family this year. They loved them!
-Elena, age 13, North Carolina

In second grade, I made a magnetic valentine for each of my classmates. These were big paper hearts with googly eyes and glitter smiles. I added a magnet on the back of each. They were so cute!
-Jenna, age 11, Illinois

A great idea is to think of an easy and sweet recipe—for example, a recipe for a heart-shaped sugar cookie—to write onto a card. You could attach a sample of the finished product to the card, or a heart shaped cookie cutter! Your friends and family will love this valentine.
-Maggie, age 9, Texas

For Valentine's Day, I always get a BIG bag of candy hearts. I make sentences out of the candy and stick them onto cards. Every year my friends love it, and it's a fun way to spend my time.
-Ashley, age 12, Delaware

I trace my hand on a piece of paper, cut it out, and fold down the two middle fingers. Then I glue it to a big heart and fold the heart in half. When someone opens it, he or she will see a hand signing, "I love you!"
-Anne, age 11, Washington

The way I show love on Valentines Day is by brightening up a special person’s day. For example, I might sit with a classmate at lunch if she is sitting by herself.
-Maria, age 10, Idaho

I make a list of all the friends and family to whom I am going to send a valentine, and I jot down a few things each person loves. Then, I cut out pictures in magazines that match what they like. I glue the scraps to a construction paper heart and fold it in half. This makes each valentine extra special for my friends and family!
-Lahni, age 12, Kentucky

This year, I made love boxes. I took little cardboard boxes from a craft store and covered them with glitter, paper, and ribbons. Then I cut out about 20 pink and red hearts for each box. On every heart I wrote something I love about that person and put it inside his or her box. My friends and family felt extra special.
-Zebba, age 11, New York

In my valentines, I like to reflect the personality of whomever I’m giving a card. For instance, if a person is really sweet, I add candy to my card. If she is silly, I’ll write a joke. It's really not that hard!
-Kiley, age 10, Connecticut

This year I'm bringing all my teachers a valentine. I glued a big peppermint patty candy to a card that thanked each teacher for helping me get a good education—and wished her or him a happy Valentine’s Day, of course!
-Becca, age 12, Michigan

I like to use doilies and multicolored gel pens for simple valentines. I don't like to buy valentines; I like to make them! I think they mean much more to my friends and family, and I have a good time making them.
-Hannah, age 11, Virginia

I love to make Valentine’s Day collages. You can cut out hearts, use pretty stamps, or glue feathers or other cool and creative materials to a cardboard heart. Hang it up or give it to someone. It’s a lot of fun!
-Jamie, age 10, Maryland

I think it is important to put yourself into the valentine. Write a poem or draw a picture—it will make the valentine unique, and the person you give it to will feel special.
-Morgan, age 11, Pennsylvania
