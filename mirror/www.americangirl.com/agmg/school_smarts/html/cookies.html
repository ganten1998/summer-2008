Why you need cookies to use Flash Card Maker
To create cards in Flash Card Maker, you will be using a "cookie." This cookie is a small file that is saved on your computer's hard drive. It contains no personal information, like your name or e-mail address, but "remembers" that your computer has visited this site before. So if you leave the site and then return, a cookie on this computer will be able to remember the last set of flash cards you made. If cookies are disabled in your browser, the last set of cards you've made will not be saved if you leave the feature and return.
Please note: You cannot access your saved set of cards from another computer. They will only be saved on the original computer you used.
