I am 12 years old and I really want to start ballet. It sounds like a lot of fun, but I’m afraid that 12 is too old to start or that I won’t be able to do anything right. Help, please! -Distressed

It’s never too late to learn. I’ve been doing ballet for seven years and I’m still learning every time. The other day, a girl came in to class and she was 16. She was really nervous and clueless, but she turned out to be really good! You should go for your dreams.
-An American girl, age 11, Arizona

First, nobody’s too old to start anything. When my sister was 13, she took a dance class. Second, you might not be able to do anything perfectly in the beginning, but that’s why you are taking a class—to improve, to learn, and to have fun.
-Marisa, age 11, Texas

I tap-dance. I just started, so I am in the beginner class, and every other girl is under the age of eight! I’ve been improving, and now the other girls look up to me. I help them when they make mistakes or if they have a question.
-An American girl, age 12, Texas

At my old dance studio, we had special “friend days” when you could bring a friend to class. If you have a friend whos taking ballet, you could tell her that you are interested in joining. You could go watch the ballet lesson and see if it would be too difficult for you. Hope this helps!
-Hannah, age 10, Texas

I had the same problem when I started basketball last year. I asked people who played basketball to teach me some of the basic shots and dribbling. I didn’t make the team, but I was happy that I tried!
-An American girl, age 11, Maryland

See if any of your friends want to be in ballet, too. If they do, ask if you can enroll in a class together.
-EmilyAlice, age 10, Iowa

I have the same exact problem, except its with karate. See if there is a ballet school close by that offers beginner classes for your age group. Don’t be afraid to start something new. Good luck!
-Meredith, age 10, Georgia

Twelve is definitely not too old to start taking ballet. The other kids probably started when they were really little, so learning the basics was much more difficult for them than it will be for you. Soon you’ll be as good as the other twelve-year-olds—and having just as much fun!
-Hannah, age 9, Texas
