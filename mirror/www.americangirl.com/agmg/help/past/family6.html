My dad thinks I like golf. The truth is I hate golf! I really don't know how to break it to him. Help me!
- Not a Golf Lover

The same exact thing happened to me. My dad signed me up for a golf clinic. I didn’t think I’d like it, but after I tried it, I loved it! Don’t be afraid to try something new, and if you don’t like it, its not a big deal. If you do like it, then you’ve just found yourself a new hobby!
-Ashley, age 11, Wisconsin

Your dad just wants to spend more time with you when he asks you to play golf. Maybe you could go to the course with him just to drive the golf cart or to hit a few balls. Make it about the together time, not about the fact that you do not like golf.
-Lisa, age 12, California

Golf could be really fun. If you try it and you still don’t like it, then you should tell your dad. He might be disappointed, but you could find another sport to play together.
-An American girl, age 10, Pennsylvania

Set aside time to talk to your dad. Tell him, “Dad, I know this is important to you, but golf just isn’t my thing.” He should understand that everyone has her own likes and dislikes.
-Shelby, age 9, Ohio

You don’t like golf, but why? Is it because its hard for you? If so, then you should try to practice and see what happens. Is it because you think it’s boring? Try to spice it up a bit. Go to a mini-golf place the next time your dad wants to go golfing with you. Just think about why you don’t like golf and what you could do to like it a little bit more.
-Helen, age 11, Pennsylvania

Make a compromise: tell your dad that you’d rather do something else but that you will golf with him once a month.
-Claire, age 12, Washington

Calmly explain to your dad why you do not like golf. I had the same problem, and my dad said that he didnt like it either! He was just playing it cause he thought that I liked it.
-Jillian, age 11, Missouri

Suggest doing other activities with your dad, like tennis or taking a nice bike ride in the park. Maybe even play soccer with him. There are hundreds of other sports. If you try different activities, you may start up new conversations. My dad and I always try to do something different when we bond.
-Rachel, age 12, Alabama
