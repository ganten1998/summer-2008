How to share a room and give space at the same time.
How to help a sister from missing mom all the time.
 

My mom is always at work. I barely ever see her—only on the weekends. What should I do? I want to spend more time with her.
-Almost mom-less!

Talk to your mom. She needs to know how you feel. Say something like, “Mom, I miss spending time with you. Is there a way we could spend more time together?” Then brainstorm things you can do together. You will feel closer in no time!
-Sarah, age 11, Arkansas

Make the most of the time you do have with your mom. Help her make dinner or clean. There’s always the weekend—try biking, hiking, or just walking together. It’s good exercise and it doesn’t take much time. Your mom loves you and wants to spend time with you, too. Remember that, and you will find time for each other.
-Leunah, age 12, California

My mom is the same way. I understand that she needs to work most of the day, but I still miss her a lot. I spoke with her and it made things much better. On the weekends, we go shopping or to the movies. I cherish the time I spend with her. I know that even though she’s gone sometimes, it doesn’t mean that she no longer loves me.
-Evangeline, age 11, Oregon

I know how you feel. Every once in a while, my mom takes me out to eat during my lunch period. It makes me feel better because she is spending time with me—even if it’s just for a little while.
-Hailey, age 9, Illinois

Give your mom an envelope filled with coupons that say, “Good for a shopping trip with me” or “Good for one batch of cookies, baked and eaten with me.” Plan to use these coupons on Fridays or any day you and your mom are free. My mom and I do this sometimes and its a lot of fun!
-Sienna, age 11, Idaho

Your mom sounds like she has a pretty busy schedule. Every mom is thrilled to spend time with her daughter, but it looks like you might have to ask in advance. Approach your mom and try to plan an outing together. Allow her to check her calendar and set a date with you. Your mom's schedule is not very flexible, but she still wants to spend time with you.
-Micheala, age 12, New Hampshire

When your mom is home, do something that she enjoys and that will make her relax. If she likes to sew, maybe she can teach you how. Or you could help her out with things, such as making lunch and dinner. Thats what I do with my mom!
-An American girl, age 10, Nebraska

My mom works full-time, too. Almost every morning, we go on a walk with our dogs at about 6:00 A.M. It gives us fresh air, exercise, and a chance to catch up. We also talk before bed at night. Good luck!
-An American girl, age 11, Illinois
