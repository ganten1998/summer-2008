Im nervous about taking showers in gym class.
How to avoid getting homesick while at camp.
 

I start middle school in August, and I'm nervous because we have to take showers in front of other girls after gym class. I really feel uncomfortable about it. Lots of girls in my grade are more developed than I am, and I'm afraid that they are going to tease me. Please help!
- Signed, Petrified
Your body is perfect just the way it is! The great thing about middle school is that there are a lot of different body types. The best thing to do is face your fears. But if you really feel uncomfortable, find out if you’re allowed to wear a bathing suit or if there are any showers with a stall. If all else fails, remember that there are good things about being small. Love your body and boost that confidence.
-Becky, age 13, Illinois

This isn't as bad as you think. One thing to remember is that no matter what they say, you are special. I was the first one to start developing of all my friends. That was embarrassing to me. I wanted to start when my friends started. Your situation is different, but the thing that helped me the most was to talk to my mom.
-Your secret friend, age 11, Mexico

I have always dreaded that, too! The best thing to do is either stay by your good friends or find people who don’t know who you are. That way you shouldn’t feel awkward.
-An AG fan, age 13, Texas

It does not matter what other people think about you. Ask yourself this: What do YOU think about yourself?
-Ashley, age 10, Illinois

Talk to your friends and the people with whom you are going to be showering, and I think you'll find that 99 percent of the other girls feel the same way! Then you can talk about it and figure out a way that you and the other girls won't feel uncomfortable.
-Chloe, age 10, California

I am going into middle school next year and have the exact same problem. However, my friend and I worked it out so that one of us will shower while the other one is holding up a towel, and then we’ll switch.
-Anna, age 11, Ohio

I had the same problem, until I got into middle school and realized something: Every girl is different. Some girls are more developed than others, but no one is exactly the same. I don't think that you have to worry about other girls teasing you. Many of them probably have the same worries, too. Now all my nervousness is gone, and I am fine. Calm down and relax about this—you’re normal!
-Glad to help, age 13, Michigan

Ask a good, trustworthy friend to shower next to you. If you do get teased, she'll be there to stand up for you.
-Meghan, age 11, California
