Im not good at keeping secrets.
I want to give good Christmas gifts, but I cant afford to.
Inexpensive summer fun ideas when family is short on money.
 

For Christmas I feel like I need to give everyone I know a present. It's hard, and if a friend gives me a present and I don't have one for her or can't afford one, I feel terrible! Sometimes I feel like a homemade present is too cheap-looking and the person will just throw it away when she gets home! HELP!
-Want to be a good gift giver

It’s O.K. if you make homemade gifts. I have made and gotten homemade gifts, and they are so important to me. It shows how much your friends care if they take the time to sit down and make a gift from the heart.
-Annie, age 10, North Carolina

Everybody loves candy! Why not ask your friends what kinds of candy they like, then go out and buy some of their favorite treats. Candy tastes good and it’s not very expensive.
-Ryan, age 12, Georgia

Your real friends won’t really care exactly what the present is or how much it cost. They will just be happy because you thought of them and gave something to them.
-Rachel, age 13, Michigan

One thing I do every Christmas is make ornaments. Making them is easy and fun to do, and they don’t look cheap. Ask your mom or dad to get supplies at a craft store. Then, when you give an ornament, you’ll feel good and your friend can hang it on a tree.
-Kelsey, age 11, Arizona

It's no surprise that you want to give everyone a gift. But in truth, friends just will want to know you care. So this year, skip the gifts and throw a party or go to the movies. Do something, like a group date that sounds like fun. That way you can give your friends something better than a homemade gift and something they will enjoy!
-Taylor, age 10, Florida

Try making stationery on the computer. Make it really cute and everybody will love getting it!
-Robyn, age 12, Alabama

Write each of your friends a poem and tell her how good of a friend she is.
-Jazzmyn, age 9, New York

Maybe you can save up your money in a little bank and call it your "Christmas Fund" or "Santa's Saver Bank." Once you have a good amount of money, you can buy a nice gift or an interesting craft kit. But just remember, no one will turn away a gift from you! Just make it with love.
-Monica, age 9, Pennsylvania
