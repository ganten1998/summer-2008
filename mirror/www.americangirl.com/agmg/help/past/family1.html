How to deal with the death of a cat.
How to share a room and give space at the same time.
How to help a sister from missing mom all the time.
 

My cat just died. I really miss him. I can't stop thinking about him. I don't want to forget him, but I don't want to constantly think about him either. How will I get through this? What should I do?
-Cat sorrow

You could keep a picture of your cat on your desk or nightstand. That way you can move on with life but not forget him.
-Melanie, age 12, Michigan

I had to put my cat to sleep recently. To remember him, we planted a tree outside in his honor. We've had some little "visits" from him where we'll hear a meow or see him sitting somewhere. Maybe your cat will give you signs like that! Good luck, and I'm sorry about your cat.
-Lindsey, age 10, Ohio

I know how you feel. My bird and hamster just died. If you feel like you want him close to you that’s normal. Keep something that he liked, such as one of his toys or his collar, and put it someplace special. Sooner or later your grieving will get better. I hope this advice helps!
-A friend, age 11, Massachusetts

For a while it will be hard to think about your cat, so keep busy with other activities. But after a while, if you're ready, think about the good times you had with your cat. You can also talk to a friend, sibling, or adult about how you are feeling. You'll be sad that your cat died, but you'll be happy about the good times you had together.
-Elaina, age 13, Indiana

Create a scrapbook for him using his pictures, name tag, favorite food label, a little bit of writing about him, and anything else you can find that relates to him. You don't have to look at it often. This way you won't have to try to remember him so hard, and the less you think about him, the easier it will be, although you'll never forget him!
-Jennifer, age 10, Florida

It sounds like you really miss your cat. Maybe you can go to a friend’s house and visit her pets. Also talk to your parents about getting another pet if you want. But remember, you never have to forget about your cat. You can always have that little space inside your heart where all the happy memories you and your cat had are kept.
-Stephanie, age 9, Illinois

My cat died, too. I know how hard it is. You can draw pictures, which might help you feel better. One day you might remember him and still be sad, but not sad enough to cry.
-Elyse, age 8, Pennsylvania

Put together a memory box of his favorite things. Put in his favorite toys, his food and water dishes, a picture of his cat food brand, and his favorite treat. And make him a homemade grave marker with one of those mix and pour sets. This will help you to deal with such a sad event better, I hope!
-MaryAnna, age 13, Texas
