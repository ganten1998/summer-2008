I need exercise ideas that are fun.

 

My doctor says I should get more exercise, but I HATE exercising! Do you know of any fun ways for me to get my daily dose?
-Weak on working out

One fun way that I exercise is I turn on the radio in my room and start dancing, stretching, working out—you name it! You won't even know that you're exercising, because you're having so much fun.
-Allison, age 10, New York

If you have dogs or cats, play with them! Run around and throw them toys. Your pets will be happy, and it really can be great exercise!
-Alana, age 12, Michigan

Do you like to swim or bike? If you do either one of those activities for an hour or two each day over the summer, then you'll have fun and get in shape for the new school year!
-Amanda, age 11, Virginia

Try being a sideline referee at a friend's sporting event. You'll run up and down the sidelines, get excited for your friend, and have fun.
-Rachel, age 9, New Jersey

Try playing hide-and-seek. It's a fun way to get yourself running. There are lots of other outside activities to play, such as freeze tag, kick the can, and Marco Polo. The list is never-ending! Play all these games with your friends and have fun!
-Jenna, age 10, West Virginia

What I do to get more exercise is make a whirlpool in my swimming pool. (This works best in a round pool or spa.) I do this by asking my family to help me. First we swim one way for about 10 minutes, and then we turn around and swim the other way. It’s really fun and great exercise.
-Erin, age 11, California

Get a group of friends together for an exercising party! That way everyone, including you, will get exercise and have fun together!
-Kayla, age 12, Ohio

One fun way to get exercise is to make a big tic-tac-toe board on the ground with chalk. In each square, put an exercise requirement, such as 20 push-ups. To get a square, you have to meet the requirement. Ask a friend to join you and play tic-tac-toe.
-Remy, age 10, Georgia
