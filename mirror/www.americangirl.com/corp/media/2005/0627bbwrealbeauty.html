AMERICAN GIRL realbeauty INSIDE AND OUT™

Introducing the American Girl Bath and Body Product Collection

New York – June 27, 2005 – Girls today face tremendous societal pressures to look, dress, and act a certain way in order to be considered beautiful. To help girls see that real beauty comes from taking good care of themselves and feeling strong, confident and happy, Bath & Body Works and American Girl are proud to announce the introduction of American Girl realbeauty Inside and Out™— a collection of engaging, high-quality, personal care products celebrating girls ages 8 to 12. The full product line, along with American Girl® magazine and a selection of American Girl’s award-winning advice books on personal care, will launch this August in all worldwide Bath & Body Works stores and at American Girl Place Chicago and New York. Special gift sets will also be available through American Girl’s award-winning Web site, americangirl.com.

The powerful melding of these two influential brands developed as a result of mutual respect.  It leverages Bath & Body Works’ market-leading equity in the fragrant body care category with one of the nation’s most trusted and respected brands for girls. According to Neil Fiske, CEO of Bath & Body Works, “American Girl is the perfect partner for this line. As the leading authority on communicating with girls and helping them celebrate their potential, American Girl has earned a loyal following of young fans, as well as the trust of their parents. Combining the strengths of American Girl with Bath & Body Works creates a ‘dream team’ of experts whose goal is to create the best personal care products that help girls feel good about themselves, inside and out.”

President of American Girl and Executive Vice President of Mattel, Ellen Brothers, further states, “Partnering with Bath & Body Works on this new personal care line is another great way for us to make a meaningful difference in the lives of young girls. We’re especially proud to join forces with a company that understands, like we do, the importance of nurturing the whole girl—her body, her mind, and her spirit.”

To help girls understand that real beauty is a spirit that comes from liking themselves inside and out, the line incorporates trusted advice from several American Girl books, including Real Beauty: 101 Ways to Feel Good About You, the best-selling Care & Keeping of You: The Body Book for Girls, and Hair: Styling Tips and Tricks for Girls. In addition, the line was developed with the feedback of more than 1,000 girls and their mothers during the past 18 months, to ensure the products were not only loved by girls, but also trusted by moms.

Packaged in engaging, multi-colored hues of berry, each product will come with its own inspirational message about “real beauty,” such as “Real beauty means no one’s smile shines exactly like yours,” or “Real beauty is helping a friend,” or “Real beauty is trusting in yourself.”  These important messages will also be incorporated in all merchandising and communications for the line.

American Girl realbeauty Inside and Out™ should be viewed as a girl’s first “real” personal care line. Each product has been formulated to be ultra-gentle using the finest, vitamin-enriched ingredients that are safe, non-irritating and effective. Additionally, each product has been dermatologist-tested to ensure the products are appropriate and beneficial for 8- to 12-year-old girls. The product line features high-use, broadly appealing fragrant body care products, as well as lip and hair careitems. Following the initial launch, a signature fragrance eau de toilette will be introduced in a custom-designed, star-shaped glass bottle in October 2005. Plans are also in place for the introduction of a facial skin care line and active sport line to debut in 2006.

Bath & Body Works is a business of Limited Brands, Inc. (NYSE:LTD). For more information, please visit our website at www.bathandbodyworks.com. To locate a store nearby, consumers can call 1.800.395.1001.

American Girl, Inc. is a wholly owned subsidiary of Mattel, Inc. (NYSE:MAT, www.mattel.com), the world’s leading toy company. Since American Girl’s inception in 1986, the company has devoted its entire business to celebrating the potential of girls ages 3 to 12. American Girl encourages girls to dream, to grow, to aspire, to create and to imagine through a wide range of engaging and insightful books, age-appropriate and educational products and unforgettable experiences. In meeting its mission with a vigilant eye toward quality and service, American Girl has earned the loyal following of millions of girls and the praise and trust of parents and educators.
