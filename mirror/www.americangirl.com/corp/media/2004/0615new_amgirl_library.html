NEW AMERICAN GIRL LIBRARY® BOOKS GIVE GIRLS A SMART START FOR SCHOOL

Middleton, WI – June 15, 2004 – Smart girls know that success in school takes hard work—and a little help! This June, to give girls 9 and up a great start to the upcoming school year, American Girl® is introducing four new back-to-school books: A Smart Girl’s Guide to Starting Middle School, Math Smarts, School Smarts Planner, and What Would You Do?

Middle School
One of the hardest things girls experience when starting middle school is not knowing what to expect—about homework, teachers, and everything in between. A Smart Girl’s Guide to Starting Middle School addresses the big questions girls have about adjusting to a new school, new teachers, new friends, and other changes middle school brings. Filled with expert advice, tips, and quizzes, A Smart Girl’s Guide to Starting Middle School helps girls approach the start of middle school with confidence and enthusiasm.

Math
Girls tend to lose interest in math as they get older. To show how much fun—and how useful—math can be, American Girl is introducing Math Smarts: Tips for Learning, Using, and Remembering Math!. Written in girl-friendly terms, Math Smarts uses practical applications to show readers that math is used all around them, such as in figuring out how much paint to buy to repaint a bedroom, or how to double a recipe. A companion book to School Smarts and Writing Smarts, this helpful guide also includes test-taking tips, homework helpers, and a handy multiplication memorization tool to get girls geared up for math.

Organization
Girls can be organized and cool at school with American Girl’s new School Smarts Planner they can personalize themselves—with slots for up to 12 friend photos on the front and 352 stickers for holidays and special days. The planner also includes quizzes, jokes, school tips, snack recipes, and craft ideas, plus a ruler, four bookmarks, tear-out notes, and helpful reference pages.

Problem Solving
In What Would You Do?: Quizzes About Real-Life Problems, girls can test their problem-solving skills by answering questions on how they would handle everyday problems, such as “What would you do if someone told a lie about you?” or “Would you tell a teacher if you knew a classmate was cheating?” Based on the solutions they pick from the choices given, girls can gather insight into what their responses reveal about them.

A Smart Girl’s Guide to Middle School, Math Smarts, School Smarts Planner, and What Would You Do? will be available in June in bookstores nationwide, at American Girl Place® in Chicago and New York, through the American Girl catalogue, and at americangirl.com. To place an order or request a free American Girl catalogue, call 1-800-845-0005.

ABOUT AMERICAN GIRL
Introduced in 1995, American Girl Library is a line of bestselling advice and activity books that reflects the spirit and interests of today’s American girl. The lively collection of books is inspired by the most popular sections of American Girl® magazine and covers such topics as friendships, health and wellness, decorating and party ideas, crafts, and school tips.

The American Girl business was founded in 1985 by Pleasant T. Rowland and was acquired by Mattel, Inc. in 1998. Deeply committed to its mission to celebrate girls, the American Girl® brand has earned the loyal following of millions of girls as well as the praise and trust of parents and educators.
