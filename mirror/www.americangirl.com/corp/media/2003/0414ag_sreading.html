AMERICAN GIRL CELEBRATES SUMMER DAYS WITH GREAT BOOKS AND THREE COOL OFFERS

Middleton, WI – April 14, 2003 – With summer only weeks away, it’s time to gear up for one of the season’s favorite pastimes—reading! For years, American Girl® has helped girls discover the magical world of reading with stories that delight, inspire, and entertain. And with more than 90 million books sold to date, American Girl is sure to have the story that’s perfect for any young reader. This April, to encourage summer reading and to help girls find the books that will expand their imaginations, American Girl is kicking off its best Summer Reading Celebration ever. The program includes three great offers: a free T-shirt, a $10 American Girl gift certificate, and a money-saving coupon.

Customers will receive a free T-shirt, featuring the message “It’s great to be an American Girl,” for every three books priced at $5.95 or more they buy through the American Girl catalogue, at americangirl.com, or at American Girl Place® in Chicago. And when customers buy any two books from The American Girls Collection® at their favorite bookstore, they’ll receive a $10 American Girl gift certificate by mail. The gift certificate can be used for any purchase from the American Girl catalogue, at americangirl.com, or at American Girl Place.

The third summer reading offer is a simple mouse click away. Between April 28 and May 31, 2003, girls can print a $1 off coupon after taking a new quiz on the Fun for Girls section of americangirl.com. The coupon is good for $1 off the purchase of any historical book in The American Girls Collection at Borders® bookstores nationwide.

The American Girls Collection books are available nationally in bookstores, at American Girl Place in Chicago, through the American Girl catalogue, and at americangirl.com. To place an order or request a free American Girl catalogue, call 1-800-845-0005.

 

ABOUT PLEASANT COMPANY
For more than 16 years, American Girl has provided beautifully made products for each stage of a girl’s development, from her preschool days of fantasy play through her tween years of self-expression and individuality. The American Girls Collection—Pleasant Company’s flagship product line—has brought history alive for millions of children ages 7 to 12. Artfully weaving together education and entertainment, the American Girls books, dolls, and accessories have shown girls how growing up has changed in some ways and stayed the same in others throughout American history.

More than 82 million American Girl books have been sold since 1986, including The American Girls Collection of historical fiction, American Girl Library® advice and activity books, the Amelia® notebooks, Angelina Ballerina™ picture books, and History Mysteries®, Wild at Heart®, and AG Fiction™ novels. Pleasant Company was founded in 1985 by Pleasant T. Rowland and was acquired by Mattel, Inc. (NYSE: MAT) in 1998. Deeply committed to its mission to educate and entertain, the American Girl brand has earned the loyal following of millions of girls and the praise and trust of parents and educators.
