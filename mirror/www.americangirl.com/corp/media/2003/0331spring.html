PLEASANT COMPANY’S FUND FOR CHILDREN ANNOUNCES SPRING GRANTS

Over $440,000 Earmarked for Arts and Environmental Programs for Area Children

Middleton, WI – March 31, 2003 – Pleasant Company’s Fund For Children is pleased to announce that $449,500 has been granted to support children’s programming throughout Dane County. The following nine Dane County organizations will receive funding for the 2003-2004 season:

Two-Year Grants

  • Aldo Leopold Nature Center received a 2-year grant totaling $200,000 for Nature Net, a collaborative consortium of natural sites in southern Wisconsin dedicated to environmental education for elementary school children. To date, the Fund for Children has contributed over $1.5 million to the Nature Center.

  • Dane County Cultural Affairs Commission (DCCAC) received a grant of $150,000 to be distributed over a two-year period to help support various cultural programs for children. This grant brings the Fund’s total contributions to DCCAC to nearly $600,000.

One-Year Grants

  • CTM Madison Family Theatre Company received $25,000 to help support the production costs of A Christmas Carol.

  • Madison Symphony Orchestra was awarded $15,000 for its Fall Youth Concert series.

  • Opera For The Young received $15,000 for its 2003 school tour of Rusalka, a mermaids tale.

  • Olbrich Botanical Society received $14,500 for a five month Youth Gardening and Environmental Education Program.

Atwood Community Foundation received $10,000 in support of Creative Connections, an arts and environmental program for children ages 3 to 18.

Madison Children’s Choir was awarded $10,000 for its spring concert and a commissioned mass piece.

Friends of Troy Gardens received $10,000 for a children’s education program on gardening, nutrition and health, and other cultural and environmental activities.

In addition to the grants noted above, Pleasant Company’s Fund For Children also provides ongoing financial support to the Madison Civic Center for Pleasant Company OnStage! A Performing Arts Series for Children, which provides world-class performances for school-age children in southern Wisconsin. The Fund For Children has supported the performing arts series since 1996, bringing its total contributions to over $1 million.

To date, the Fund has awarded over $4 million to numerous Dane County organizations. Funding is made possible through the Madison Children’s Museum annual benefit sale of second and returned American Girl products. A portion of the benefit sale proceeds is returned to Pleasant Company’s Fund for Children and redistributed to support partnerships with several nonprofit groups serving the arts, culture, and environment.

The next deadline for submitting Fund For Children grant applications is August 1, 2003. Grant decisions are made approximately eight weeks after the deadline. To request a grant application, please call the Madison Community Foundation at 232-1763.
