THE AMERICAN GIRLS REVUE DEBUTS AT AMERICAN GIRL PLACE NEW YORK®

Live Musical Brings the Popular American Girl Characters and Stories to Life

Middleton, WI – November 3, 2003 – If you’re in the market for theater for children that’s sincere and affirming, charming and sweet, look no further than The American Girls Revue—a live musical performed in the 130-seat American Girl Theater, located inside New York’s own American Girl Place retail and entertainment site at 609 Fifth Avenue. The performance, written by Broadway playwright Gretchen Cryer and composer Nancy Ford, is inspired by the beloved American Girl characters—Kaya, Felicity, Josefina, Kirsten, Addy, Kit, Samantha, and Molly—nine-year-old fictional heroines whose stories are set during important times in America’s past. The American Girls Revue, which enjoyed an impressive three-year run at the company’s Chicago site, officially opens at American Girl Place New York on Friday, November 21, with previews beginning November 12, 2003.

The one-hour musical, which is performed by a cast of eight girls and four adults, tells the story of a group of modern-day girls at a meeting of their American Girls club. Each girl has prepared to act out her favorite American Girls story for a newcomer who is not familiar with the characters. As the girls relive the various characters’ experiences, they establish the underlying theme of the play: Look to the past; learn for the future. As each girl in turn brings her favorite story to life and steps into the role of an American Girls heroine, she gains new found awareness of her own strengths, her own voice, and her own feelings. The finale, “The American Girls Anthem” celebrates the girls’ resolve to apply the lessons from the past to their own lives as they sing about what it means to be an American girl of today.

“The American Girls Revue embodies the same spirit that lies at the heart of the entire American Girl brand—celebrating girls of yesterday and today,” says Scott Davidson, American Girl Theater director and the show’s executive producer. “By striking the right tone between simplicity and sophistication, the show inspires and delights children and adults, while sending a powerful message to today’s girls that this is their moment in history to shine.”

Davidson is joined by a first-rate creative team, including Choreographer Linda Cholodenko, musical director Jamie Schmidt, scenic designer Ken Goldstein, costume designer Virgil C. Johnson, lighting designer Camille Connolly, and sound designer Michael Bodeen.

Out of the hundreds who auditioned for this demanding theatrical opportunity, a total of 36 girls and 8 adults were selected to play the various roles. The American Girls Revue is performed on the following days and times:

  • Fridays—Two performances: 5:30 & 7:00 p.m.

  • Saturdays—Five performances: 12:30, 2:00, 4:00, 5:30 p.m. & 7:00 p.m.

  • Sundays—Four performances: 12:30, 2:00, 4:00, & 5:30 p.m.

To order tickets for The American Girls Revue or for more information, call toll free 1-877-AG PLACE. The performance schedule is subject to change in response to demand and during holidays. American Girl Theater is located inside New York’s premiere retail and entertainment site, American Girl Place, located at 609 Fifth Avenue, across from Rockefeller Center.

 

ABOUT PLEASANT COMPANY
Headquartered in Middleton, Wisconsin, Pleasant Company opened American Girl Place Chicago, its first retail and entertainment site, in November 1998. To date, the Chicago store has welcomed over 6 million visitors, served more than 700,000 meals in the Cafe, and seated more than 300,000 theatergoers. American Girl Place Chicago has received numerous awards, including the Themed Entertainment Award (THEA) for best overall themed retail entertainment destination in the world.

Pleasant Company was founded in 1985 by Pleasant T. Rowland and was acquired by Mattel, Inc. (NYSE: MAT) in 1998. Deeply committed to its mission to educate and entertain, the American Girl brand has earned the loyal following of millions of girls and the praise and trust of parents and educators.
