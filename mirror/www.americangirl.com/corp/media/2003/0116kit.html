AMERICAN GIRL FANS ‘RIDE THE RAILS’ WITH KIT IN A NEW TRAVEL ADVENTURE

Innovative Art Studio Books Also Spark Creative Fun with the American Girls

Middleton, WI – January 16, 2003 – This March, girls can join Kit Kittredge—one of the beloved characters from The American Girls Collection®—as she embarks on a railway adventure from Cincinnati to Montana’s Glacier National Park in 1934. Kit’s Railway Adventure features an interactive journal-style format that encourages girls’ imaginations and gives fans a new way to further explore the world of one of the most popular American Girls characters.

Kit’s Railway Adventure begins after Kit wins an essay contest and an all-expense paid train trip for two to anywhere in the country. Kit and her Aunt Millie choose to head west to see Montana’s beautiful Glacier Park—and Kit’s brother Charlie, who’s working for the Civilian Conservation Corps (CCC). Along the way, Kit and Aunt Millie stop to ride the Sky Ride and see the World’s Largest Fountain at the “Century of Progress” World’s Fair in Chicago, stay overnight at a cattle ranch, and meet the President and First Lady, Franklin and Eleanor Roosevelt. To bring Kit’s adventure to life, the hardcover book includes historical photographs and eight pull-out souvenirs for girls to keep, such as a map and a bookmark from the 1934 World’s Fair, a train whistle, a Native American bracelet, and a secret decoder girls can use to figure out clues hidden throughout the book.

More Creative Fun with the American Girls!
Other new spring books designed to deepen girls’ experience with the American Girls and spark creativity include four interactive Art Studio Kits based on the Kaya™, Kit, Molly®, and Samantha characters. Each self-contained art kit features 24 detailed line scenes from the character’s six books, as well as extra pages with decorative borders for personal designs and a storage envelope to hold works in progress. The kits also include six watercolor pencils, a special blending tool, and an “Artist Tips and Tricks” guide with directions for shading, shadows, and other techniques. And for girls eager to read more adventures about their favorite historical characters, seven new American Girls Short Stories™ will also debut in March 2003: Kaya and the River Girl, Thanks to Josefina, Kirsten’s Promise, Addy’s Summer Place, Samantha’s Special Talent, Kit’s Tree House, and, Molly’s Puppy Tale. Each 56-page pocket-sized hardcover book includes a historical “Looking Back” picture essay as well as a craft or activity related to the story.

The American Girls Collection books are available nationally in bookstores, at American Girl Place® in Chicago, through the American Girl catalogue, and at americangirl.com. To place an order or request a free American Girl catalogue, call 1-800-845-0005.

 

ABOUT PLEASANT COMPANY
For more than 16 years, American Girl has provided beautifully made products for each stage of a girl’s development, from her preschool days of fantasy play through her ‘tween’ years of self-expression and individuality. The American Girls Collection—Pleasant Company’s flagship product line—has brought history alive for millions of children ages 7 to 12. Artfully weaving together education and entertainment, the American Girls books, dolls, and accessories have shown girls how growing up has changed in some ways and stayed the same in others throughout American history.

More than 82 million American Girl books have been sold since 1986, including The American Girls Collection of historical fiction, American Girl Library® advice and activity books, the Amelia® notebooks, Angelina Ballerina™ picture books, and History Mysteries®, Wild at Heart™, and AG Fiction™ novels. Pleasant Company was founded in 1985 by Pleasant T. Rowland and was acquired by Mattel, Inc. (NYSE: MAT) in 1998. Deeply committed to its mission to educate and entertain, the American Girl brand has earned the loyal following of millions of girls and the praise and trust of parents and educators.
