AMERICAN GIRL® MAGAZINE ANNOUNCES COVER CONTEST WINNERS

Contest Garnered More Than 6800 Entries from Girls Across the Country!

Middleton, WI – October 24, 2003 – American Girl magazine—the country’s most popular magazine dedicated exclusively to girls ages 8 and up—caps off its festive, year-long 10th birthday celebration with the release of the November/December 2003 issue showcasing the six winners of its nationwide Cover Contest.

Announced in American Girl’s January/February 2003 issue, the Cover Contest—which asked girls ages 8 to 13 to show how they represent the true spirit of American Girl—was the magazine’s most popular contest to date. More than 6800 entries, including videos, craft and sewing projects, essays, and scrapbooks, were sent in from girls across the country.

“We were overwhelmed by the response to the Cover Contest,” said Kristi Thom, Executive Editor of American Girl magazine. “With so many wonderful entries, it made judging them incredibly difficult. But it was gratifying to hear from many girls and their parents how much they enjoyed working on their projects, and we hope that all the girls who participated learned something about themselves in the process.”

Inside the special reversible issue, readers will meet the six cover girls and find out what made their winning entries stand out with the judges. In addition, the article features a behind-the-scenes glimpse at what the girls did on and off the set during the two day photo shoot in Chicago—from professional hair and wardrobe styling to a fun-filled celebratory dinner at legendary dining spot, Ed Debevic’s.

American Girl magazine is available nationally in retail bookstores and on newsstands, as well as by subscription through the American Girl catalogue. Call 1-800-234-1278 for subscription information.

 

ABOUT AMERICAN GIRL MAGAZINE
Created as an age-appropriate alternative to teen magazines, American Girl—an award-winning, bi-monthly publication—is rich with colorful illustrations and photography, award-winning fiction and nonfiction, games, crafts, and party ideas. In addition to articles about real girls and their achievements, the magazine includes regular departments like “Help!” and “Heart to Heart” that invite reader participation and allow girls to share advice and ideas about issues that concern them.

Since its launch in 1992, the magazine’s circulation has grown to more than 650,000 subscribers and is ranked among the top 10 children’s magazines in the nation and is the largest magazine dedicated exclusively to girls.

Pleasant Company, creator of the American Girl brand, was founded in 1985 by Pleasant T. Rowland and was acquired by Mattel, Inc. (NYSE: MAT) in 1998.
