AMELIA® SHARES A FEW LIFE SKILLS WITH GIRLS
—New Titles Explore the Complexities of Family Relationships—

Middleton, WI (September 2001)— This fall, Amelia, the perpetually inquisitive and talented artist, discovers that being a girl isn’t always easy in Oh Boy, Amelia!, her latest notebook from American Girl®. Told with wisdom and humor, Oh Boy, Amelia! teaches girls about the importance of expressing their individuality and encourages them to pursue activities that promote their own talents and interests.

Amelia is confused when Cleo, her normally smart (but obnoxious) older sister, starts acting brainless and "girly" around her new boyfriend, Oliver. In typical little-sister fashion, Amelia spies on the couple and finds out that Cleo has purposely altered her personality in hopes that Oliver will ask her to the school dance. At the same time, Amelia enrolls in a co-ed Life Skills class at school, where she learns that trying to fit into others’ expectations of proper girl behavior can lead to problems. In the end, Amelia figures out that being yourself and doing what you enjoy is the key to happiness. As Amelia says, "It’s not whether it’s a girl thing or a boy thing that matters, but whether it’s YOUR thing."

Created by award-winning author Marissa Moss, Amelia’s notebooks entertain readers with great stories about the ups and downs of growing up, while encouraging girls to express their creativity by drawing and writing about their own lives. Amelia’s stories first appeared in American Girl® magazine in 1995 and continue to be a favorite feature among the publication’s 640,000 subscribers, who regard her as a role model and friend. Pleasant Company Publications acquired the rights to the Amelia titles in January 1999, and has since sold more than 1.2 million Amelia notebooks. Marissa Moss has written and illustrated more than 20 children’s books. Her books, many of which draw from her own experiences, have won numerous awards from educational organizations.

Amelia’s notebooks are available nationally in bookstores, at American Girl Place® in Chicago, through the American Girl catalogue, and at www.americangirl.com. To place an order or request a free American Girl catalogue, call 1-800-845-0005.

More than 74 million American Girl books have been sold since 1986, including The American Girls Collection® of historical fiction, American Girl Library® advice and activity books, Amelia’s notebooks, Angelina Ballerina™ picture books, and History Mysteries®, Wild at Heart™, and AG Fiction™ novels.

Pleasant Company was founded in 1985 by Pleasant T. Rowland and became a subsidiary of Mattel, Inc. (NYSE: MAT) in 1998. The mission of Pleasant Company’s core brand, American Girl, is to educate and entertain girls with high-quality products and experiences that reinforce positive social and moral values.
