AMERICAN GIRLS TRAVEL BOOKS TAKE GIRLS ON A WHOLE NEW ADVENTURE
—New Interactive Journals Feature Historical Photos and Real-Life Souvenirs—

Middleton, WI (March 2002)— Among many cherished American traditions, none is more celebrated than the family trip. This spring, girls can join two of their favorite characters from The American Girls Collection®—Molly McIntire® and Samantha Parkington™—as they depart on two exciting family adventures: a 1940s road trip on Route 66 and a trans-Atlantic cruise aboard an elegant ocean liner in the early 1900s.

To bring these adventures to life, each hardcover, journal-style book features historical photographs and eight pull-out souvenirs for girls to keep. The interactive format encourages girls' imaginations and gives them a fresh new way to explore the worlds of their favorite American Girls characters.

In Molly's Route 66 Adventure, girls ride along with Molly and her family as they make their way from Chicago to Los Angeles on the now-legendary Route 66. Through Molly's first-person account of the trip, readers imagine having a double ice cream soda at a quirky roadside diner, staying up late for a drive-in movie, and riding a burro to the bottom of the Grand Canyon. The book's interactive souvenirs include items like a Route 66 map, a diner menu, accordion postcards, a bandit mask, and a secret decoder that lets girls unscramble clues to help Molly win a prize in Royal Oil's famous Hollywood Mystery Code Contest.

In Samantha's Ocean Liner Adventure, girls set sail with Samantha aboard the glorious S.S. Londonia as it journeys across the Atlantic Ocean to England. Readers will imagine the grandness of the mighty steamer ship as they read about Samantha having high tea in an elegant garden, eating fancy food, and dancing at the Captain's Ball. Samantha's trip below the ship's decks to the steerage cabins, however, opens readers' eyes to the realities of a very different world. Girls can play with the book's eight pull-out souvenirs, including a map of the ship, a telegram, a mask from the Captain's Ball, and a Morse Code card to decode secret messages from Samantha.

To further bring Molly and Samantha's travel adventures to life this March, American Girl will introduce new historically accurate travel outfits and accessories for the 18-inch Molly and Samantha dolls. Molly's blue linen dress with eyelet trim and flower-shaped buttons is cool and comfortable for her trip down Route 66, and Samantha's ivory duster and scarf keep her warm on the ocean liner's deck.

Beginning March 11, girls can also participate in Molly and Samantha travel-related Web activities at www.americangirl.com. Games like Count the Miles and Backseat Bingo tie in to Molly's road travel theme. And Samantha's activity, Morse Code Messages, lets girls send family and friends coded messages just like ocean liner passengers would have in the early 1900s.

The travel journals and other books from American Girl are available nationally in bookstores, at American Girl Place® in Chicago, through the American Girl catalogue, and at www.americangirl.com. To place an order or request a free American Girl catalogue, call 1-800-845-0005.
