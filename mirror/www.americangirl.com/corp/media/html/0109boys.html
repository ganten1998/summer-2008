NEW AMERICAN GIRL BOOK OFFERS `TWEEN GIRLS ADVICE ON THE "B" WORD—BOYS!
—New Book Encourages Girls to Stay True to Themselves—

Middleton, WI (September 2001)— What’s the No. 1 topic of conversation for girls ages 10 and up? The same subject that has captivated girls throughout history: boys. And today’s girls have a lot of questions when it comes to dealing with the pressure-filled, stomach-churning days of "who likes who." What do you say to a boy? How can you tell if a boy likes you? What about boy–girl parties? And what about rejection? This September, to help guide girls through this emotional and often confusing time, American Girl—known by millions for providing straightforward, parent-approved advice for preteen girls—will introduce a new self-help book, A Smart Girl’s Guide to Boys: Surviving Crushes, Staying True to Yourself, and Other Love Stuff.

Unlike many teen titles on the market that focus on dating and romance, A Smart Girl’s Guide to Boys provides just the right information on girl–boy relationships in a warm, wise, and humorous way. It’s sound advice will help make growing up a little easier for girls—not to mention their parents. In addition to sparking conversation about what can be an uncomfortable topic in many households, the insightful, age-appropriate guide will also help parents send an important message to their daughters: Stay true to yourself!

Designed for girls ages 10 and up, A Smart Girl’s Guide to Boys is written in response to questions raised in hundreds of girls’ letters to American Girl® magazine. The new guide includes some of these letters, along with tips, quizzes, and even advice from boys themselves.

Another new advice title debuting from American Girl Library this fall is The Care and Keeping of Me: The Body Book Journal. The perfect companion to the bestselling title The Care and Keeping of You: The Body Book for Girls, the new journal allows girls to record their moods and keep in touch with their overall health and well-being. Also included are tips, quizzes, and checklists to help girls express the physical and emotional changes they are experiencing.

In addition to A Smart Girl’s Guide to Boys and The Care and Keeping of Me, other new American Girl Library advice and activity books debuting this September include:

Me and My Dog: The Care and Keeping of a Girl’s Best Friend. This unique friendship book will strengthen the bond between a girl and her dog. From quizzes and how-to’s to training tips and expert advice, girls will learn how to better understand their four-legged friends. The book includes a special keepsake section for memories and pictures.

Room for You: Find Your Style and Make Your Room Say You! The first style guide created just for girls! By taking detailed quizzes, girls will discover more about their personal style and tastes and find simple, inexpensive decorating ideas that reflect their personality. Also included are special sections on organization and feng shui, plus a "room doctor" to help with familiar frustrations like sharing a room and cleaning.

Salt Dough! This creative craft book puts a clever spin on common baking ingredients: flour, water, and salt. Girls can have hours of fun learning to make inexpensive and incredibly cute miniatures, room decorations, and other keepsakes out of salt dough.
