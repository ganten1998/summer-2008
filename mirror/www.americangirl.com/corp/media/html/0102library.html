NEW AMERICAN GIRL LIBRARY BOOKS ENCOURAGE GIRLS TO TAP INTO THEIR CREATIVE SIDE

Middleton, WI (March 2001)— At American Girl, we know girls—and we know what they love to do. As a result, this spring's American Girl Library lineup of books is filled with clever crafts, fun projects, and revealing quizzes girls ages 8 to 12 will love. Whether it's the new activity books like Paper Punch Art, Letter Art, and My Life! Scrapbook Kit, or books that get girls writing like the American Girl Diary, The Quiz Book 2: More Secrets Revealed, and The Big Book of Notes, all will spark creativity while celebrating individuality and spirit.

Paper Punch Art, Letter Art, and My Life! include everything girls need to enjoy fun-filled craft activities, including paper punches, colorful paper, and stencils. Clear instructions make it easy for beginning crafters to successfully complete projects, helping them build confidence along the way. While each book is fun on its own, girls can use Paper Punch Art and Letter Art together to create scrapbook pages, invitations, and school projects.

Young writers will also be drawn to the American Girl Diary, with its soft suede-like cover and colorful illustrations that will inspire their daydreams and doodles. Each page includes whimsical and thought-provoking writing prompts, such as "If you could change your name today, what would it be?" and "Name one good quality for every member of your family," plus lots of room for girls to write about what's on their minds and in their hearts. Lighthearted books like The Quiz Book 2 and The Big Book of Notes will have girls discovering more about themselves and their friends through interactive quizzes, fortune-tellers, and fill-in-the-blank notes.

Over 8 million American Girl Library® advice and activity books have been sold since the imprint debuted in 1995. Other best-selling titles from American Girl Library include The Care & Keeping of You: The Body Book for Girls; The Care and Keeping of Friends; Pages and Pockets: A Portfolio of Secrets and Stuff; Games and Giggles—Just for Girls; and Oops! The Manners Guide for Girls. American Girl® magazine, the inspiration for the American Girl Library books, boasts a circulation of 700,000 and is read by girls ages 8 to 12 who enjoy its focus on their issues, activities, and interests.

The American Girl Library books are available nationally in bookstores, through American Girl Place® in Chicago and the American Girl catalogue, and at www.americangirl.com. For a free consumer catalogue, call 1-800-845-0005.

More than 61 million American Girl books have been sold since 1986, including The American Girls Collection® of historical fiction, American Girl Library® advice and activity books, Amelia's notebooks, and History Mysteries® and AG Fiction™ novels. Pleasant Company was founded in 1985 by Pleasant T. Rowland, and became a subsidiary of Mattel, Inc. (NYSE: MAT) in 1998. The mission of Pleasant Company's leading brand, American Girl, is to educate and entertain girls with high-quality products and experiences that reinforce positive social and moral values.
