AMERICAN GIRL DEBUTS HOPSCOTCH HILL SCHOOL™ FOR GIRLS 4 TO 6
New Early Reader Collection Builds Skills, Confidence, and Enthusiasm for Learning

Middleton, WI – August 13, 2003 – There are few things as eagerly anticipated in a child’s life as the first day of school. While there is plenty to look forward to about this milestone, such as buying school supplies and meeting the new teacher, the first day also brings new—and sometimes difficult—challenges. To help girls ages 4 to 6 greet school with confidence and eager anticipation, American Girl is introducing Hopscotch Hill School, a new line of integrated early reader books, high-quality 16-inch dolls, and classroom-themed play sets, this September.

Created in partnership with an advisory board of educational and child development experts, the Hopscotch Hill School books and products focus on teaching soft learning skills—such as sharing, persistence, and patience—that will help build the type of confidence and enthusiasm young girls can call upon for a lifetime.

The new books, written by renowned children’s author Valerie Tripp, bring the fictional Hopscotch Hill School to life through the stories of ten lively children, whose school experiences help prepare young girls for familiar classroom challenges. The first three books, Hallie’s Horrible Handwriting; Thank You, Logan!; and Bright, Shiny Skylar, are written for children who are beginning to read independently, while the engaging plots and charming watercolor illustrations will also appeal to younger girls. Each book features a “Dear Parents” section that is full of ideas and activities created by teachers and child specialists to help parents nurture their children’s skills and lifelong love of learning.

The lively characters are brought to life with beautifully crafted 16-inch Hallie™, Logan™, and Skylar™ dolls whose posable heads and bendable arms and legs make them perfect for acting out scenes from the books. Each doll retails for $48 and comes dressed in a fun school outfit with an accompanying paperback book. The detailed play sets, such as the Flowerpot Desk, Dress-up Trunk, and Rainbow Activity Board also help to re-create the magical world of Hopscotch Hill School.

The Hopscotch Hill School books, dolls, and accessories will be available this September through the American Girl catalogue, at American Girl Place®, and at americangirl.com. For a free consumer catalogue, call 1-800-845-0005. As with the other American Girl books, the Hopscotch Hill School series will also be available in bookstores throughout the country.

ABOUT PLEASANT COMPANY
For over 16 years, Pleasant Company—a direct marketer, retailer, and children’s publisher—has provided beautifully made products for each stage of a girl’s development, from her preschool days of fantasy play through her tween years of self-expression and individuality. The flagship product line, The American Girls Collection, has brought history to life for millions of girls ages 7 to 12 through its charming books, dolls, and accessories. In addition to The American Girls Collection, Pleasant Company is nationally known for the following brands: American Girl Today, Bitty Baby, Angelina Ballerina, AG Mini*s, Girls of Many Lands®, and now Hopscotch Hill School. The company also publishes bestselling books from American Girl Library®, Amelia®, History Mysteries®, AG Fiction™, and Wild at Heart™, as well as the award-winning magazine, American Girl®.

To date, more than 90 million American Girl books and 8 million dolls have been sold. Pleasant Company was founded in 1985 by Pleasant T. Rowland and was acquired by Mattel, Inc. (NYSE: MAT) in 1998. Deeply committed to its mission to educate and entertain, the American Girl brand has earned the loyal following of millions of girls and the praise and trust of parents and educators.
