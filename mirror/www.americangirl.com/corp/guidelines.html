Books

American Girl Publications publishes books for girls in the 7-13 age range. We welcome non-fiction submissions from authors and agents. We do not accept submissions in the field of fiction.

Magazine

American Girl® is a bimonthly, four-color magazine for girls ages 8 and up. The mission of American Girl magazine is to encourage girls to express themselves and achieve their personal best.
