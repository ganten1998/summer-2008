Thank you for your interest in writing for Pleasant Company Publications.

Pleasant Company Publications publishes books for girls in the 7-13 age range. We welcome submissions from authors and agents in the fields of fiction and nonfiction, as described below.


The American Girls Collection® is Pleasant Company's best-known historical fiction imprint. The books in this Collection, which tell the stories of American girls who lived long ago, include the stories about Felicity, Josefina, Kirsten, Addy, Samantha, and Molly. We rely on our own team of writers, editors, and designers for these titles, so we are not seeking manuscripts for existing characters' series or for future characters. Please do not submit ideas or manuscripts for The American Girls Collection.


Pleasant Company seeks manuscripts for AG Fiction™, its contemporary middle-grade fiction imprint. These novels capture the spirit and voices of contemporary American girls and feature an 11-to-13-year-old protagonist who clearly demonstrates the hopes, thoughts, and emotions unique to that precarious age between childhood and teenhood. We are looking for thoughtfully developed characters and plots and a discernible sense of place.

While we do not shy away from serious issues, we want AG Fiction to offer an alternative to the graphic content found in much of today's YA literature. These novels are for middle-grade readers who are not ready to delve too deeply into mature themes such as sexuality or deviant behavior.

Submit a complete manuscript of 25,000-40,000 words that meets these guidelines:

  • Features an American girl or girls, ages 11-13. Characters may be of any cultural background or family situation.
  • Depicts smart, resourceful girls who face challenges with courage or ingenuity.
  • No first-romance stories.
  • Written for 5th grade reading level.


Pleasant Company publishes advice and activity books for girls under the American Girl Library® imprint. These four-color books, densely illustrated with art and photographs, cover subjects of interest or concern to girls 8Ð12. Recent titles include:

  • Groom Your Room: Terrific Touches to Brighten Your Bedroom
  • Super Slumber Parties
  • Oops! The Manners Guide for Girls
  • The Care and Keeping of Friends
  • Clever Letters: Fun Ways to Wiggle Your Word

Our readers are girls in their formative years. We hope to encourage their dreams and to reinforce their self-confidence and curiosity as they prepare to navigate adolescence in the years ahead. Pleasant Company Publications invites proposals for:

  • Well-focused concepts for activity books, craft books, or advice books.
  • Nonfiction specifically targeted to girls. If the approach would appeal to boys as well as to girls, it is not right for American Girl Library.

Proposals should include a detailed description of your concept, sample chapters or spreads, and lists or samples of previous publications. Complete manuscripts are also acceptable.
