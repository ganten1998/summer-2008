Nonfiction

American Girl publishes advice and activity books for girls under the American Girl Library® imprint. These four-color books, densely illustrated with art and photographs, cover subjects of interest or concern to girls 8-12. Recent titles include:

  • Christmas Crafts

  • The Quiz Book 3

  • Smart Girl's Guide to Friendship Troubles

  • Micro Minis

  • Skin & Nails: Care Tips for Girls

  • Brain Waves Puzzle Book

Our readers are girls in their formative years. We hope to encourage their dreams and to reinforce their self-confidence and curiosity as they prepare to navigate adolescence in the years ahead. American Girl Publications invites proposals for:

  • Well-focused concepts for activity books, craft books, or advice books.

  • Nonfiction specifically targeted to girls. If the approach would appeal to boys as well as to girls, it is not right for American Girl Library.

Proposals should include a detailed description of your concept, sample chapters or spreads, and lists or samples of previous publications. Complete manuscripts are also acceptable.
