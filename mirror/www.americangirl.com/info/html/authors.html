Valerie Tripp
 Author of several books in The American Girls Collection

Valerie Tripp has written all of the books in the Felicity, Josefina, Kit, and Molly series and three of the books in the Samantha series in The American Girls Collection. Valerie became a writer because she loves reading and stories. When people ask her what they should do to become a writer, she says, "Read everything anything, as much as you can - just read." Valerie lives in Maryland with her husband and daughter. Valerie will visit the following bookstores this fall.

Kathleen Ernst
 Author of Trouble at Fort LaPointe
Kathleen Ernst grew up in Maryland in a house full of books. Family vacations always included visits to historical sites and used bookstores. She wrote her first historical novel when she was fifteen, and was hooked! She’s been writing about history ever since and earned a master’s degree in history education and writing. Today she lives in Wisconsin with her husband and two cats. In addition to writing, she produces educational videos for Public Television. Her History Mystery title, Trouble at Fort LaPointe was nominated for the 2001 Edgar Award for Best Children’s Mystery.

Elizabeth McDavid Jones
 Author of Watcher in the Piney Woods, Secrets on 26th Street, and The Night Flyer
Elizabeth McDavid Jones grew up in North Carolina and still resides there, not far from the small coastal town where the famous pirate Blackbeard once lived. The beautiful beaches and barrier islands of the Carolinas are a favorite vacation spot for the McDavid-Jones family, where they enjoy swimming, sunning, surfing, shelling, and kayaking. Her History Mystery title The Night Flyers won the 2000 Edgar Allan Poe Award for Best Children’s Mystery.

Kathryn Reiss
Author of Riddle of the Prairie Bride

Kathryn Reiss grew up dreaming of being transported back to the nineteenth century. She always had her nose stuck in a book - usually a mystery. When she couldn't find anything the slightest bit eerie or criminal happening in her neighborhood, she started writing stories about mysteries she wished she would uncover. Kathryn lives with her husband, three children, two cats, and dog in a 130-year-old house in northern California.

Holly Hughes
Author of Hoofbeats of Danger
When Holly Hughes was growing up, she loved horses but rarely got to ride. Instead, she devoured mysteries and historical fiction. She also enjoyed taking car trips with her family to different parts of the country. Today, she puts all those interests to work. She writes and edits fiction for young readers, as well as travel guides to places around the world. She lives with her husband, daughter, two sons, and their cat in Manhattan, where she can look out her window and watch horses cantering through Central Park.
