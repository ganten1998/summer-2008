Dear Parent or Guardian:
Your daughter would like to participate in American Girl's online Fan Gallery by sending us a photograph and testimonial that we may post on

You must supply us with written consent—by completing the form below—before we can post photographs of your daughter. For privacy reasons, subjects in the photos will be identified by no more than first name, age, and state.

We at AmericanGirl.com care about your child's personal privacy and we encourage you to review our Privacy Policy. We have adopted special procedures to protect the privacy of children under age 13 who visit our site, in accordance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

To allow your daughter to submit a photograph and testimonial, please complete this form and sign, date, and return it by mail to the address listed in the printable instructions. Please do not send images color-printed or copied on regular paper! Images must be either original photos or printed on photo quality paper.

Your daughter's testimonial is important. We use the comments your daughter sends in as a caption to her photograph, explaining what's going on in the photo or why it is a special moment for her.

Sending us a photo, testimonial, and release form does not guarantee that your daughter will appear on the AmericanGirl.com site. We reserve the right to choose the photos and descriptions we post. No photos will be considered for posting without a signed release form. Submissions chosen may not appear on the site for several months. Materials you send will not be returned.
