What You Need
We recommend these plug-ins.

  • Shockwave: allows you to see animation, hear some sounds, and play a lot of games at AmericanGirl.com, like Kit's Money Matters, Josefina's Making Music, Samantha's Sketchbook, Picture Pieces, and more!
  • Flash: allows you to see animation, hear some sounds, and play some games at AmericanGirl.com, like quizzes in Quiz Corner, e-cards in E-Card Central. This plug-in comes with the Shockwave plug-in but can also be downloaded separately.
  • QuickTime: plays video clips, sounds, and music.
  • JavaScript: allows your browser to display rollovers and other fancy tricks.
  • Acrobat Reader: lets you view and print Portable Document Files or PDFs. On this site we'll create PDFs to give you better looking printouts of such things as certificates and other printable materials.

What You Have
You can tell what you have on your computer two ways.

  1. Visit Test the Plug-ins to see if you already have the plug-ins loaded.
  2. Check within your browser. For help visit What Do I Have?.

What You Can Get
If you discover you need plug-ins, visit Get the Plug-ins. Before you download anything, please ask your parents for permission!

How long does it take?
If your modem speed is 14.4 or 28.8, these plug-ins may take up to half an hour to download. It may seem like a long time, but the result should be worth the time. And you only have to do it once!
