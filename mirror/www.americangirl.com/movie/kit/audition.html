Kit Kittridge is coming to the big screen

...and your favorite girl could play a role

The Samantha, Felicity, and Molly movies entertained millions of girls and their families. Next summer, it’s Kit’s turn! Her new live-action feature film, Kit Kittredge: An American Girl Mystery, comes to theaters everywhere July 2, 2008. Your girl could play a supporting role alongside Academy-Award nominee Abigail Breslin, who is starring as Kit!

American Girl is hosting open auditions to recruit actresses for the new feature film.

Who we’re looking for:

All girls ages 8 to 12 (must be 8 by May 29 and cannot be 13 before this date) are welcome to audition. Girls must be available for filming in Toronto, Canada from May 29 through July 13, 2007 (availability may also be necessary for pre-production from May 21 through May 28).

When and where:

  • Monday, May 7, American Girl Place Los Angeles
  • Tuesday, May 8, American Girl Place Chicago
  • Monday, May 14, American Girl Place New York

For all locations, girls should arrive at the following times based on the first letter of their last names: A - I, 2 to 4 p.m.; J - P, 4 to 6 p.m.; Q - Z, 6 to 8 p.m. (all times local)

What to download and bring to the audition:

  • Release form – be sure to complete and sign it
  • Open casting application/call sheet – complete a profile of your girl, including contact information
  • Audition script – prepare for the audition with these scenes from the movie (memorize if possible)
  • Callback script – if your girl is selected for a callback, review this scene to prepare

A star will be born—is your girl the one? Come find out!

Kit Kittredge is a girl growing up in 1934 during the Great Depression. Her family is struggling, yet along with hard times come good times and good friends. See her new adventures on the big screen in Kit Kittredge: An American Girl Mystery — in theaters everywhere July 2, 2008.
