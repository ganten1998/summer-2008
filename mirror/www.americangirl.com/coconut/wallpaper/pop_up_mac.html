MAC - OS9

  1. When the image finishes loading in the browser window, click and hold on the image. Choose "Download Image to Disk," and save to your hard drive.
  2. In your Appearance Control Panel, select the "Desktop" tab. Click "Place Picture," choose the downloaded image, and click "Open." Choose "Set Desktop."

MAC - OSX

  1. When the image finishes loading in the browser window, click and hold on the image. Choose "Download Image to Disk," and save it to your desktop.
  2. Open your System Preferences panel and select "Desktop."
  3. Drag the downloaded image from your desktop into the well in the Desktop panel.
