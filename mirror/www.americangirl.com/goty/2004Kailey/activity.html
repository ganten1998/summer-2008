Supplies

You will need:

  • pencil
  • ruler
  • scissors
  • thick paper or poster board
  • plastic wrap or a large freezer bag
  • clear tape
  • clear-drying tape or glue stick
  • dark-colored construction paper

Optional:

  • decorative paper hole punches
  • decorative-edged scissors

Directions

1. Use your pencil and ruler to trace a large square
onto the thick paper or poster board. Then cut it out.

2. On the back of your square, use your ruler to draw
an "X" connecting the corners of the paper. From each
corner, measure the same distance (such as 2 or 3
inches) and mark it on from each corner of the "X."
Once you have all your measurements made, connect
the marks with your ruler so that it forms another square.

3. In the middle of the paper, use your pencil to carefully
poke through the paper. Use this hole to begin cutting out
the inside square. When you are done, it should look like a
frame.

4. Measure a piece of plastic wrap to fit across the open
square, or cut a large freezer bag apart to use. On the
backside of the thick paper, tape the plastic down so that
it looks like a window inside your frame.

5. Tear up the tissue paper into large pieces. Crumple the
paper and then smooth it out flat again. Glue or tape down
the pieces of the tissue paper onto the backside of the thick
paper. Layer the pieces as you go.

6. Once you have the entire plastic window covered with
tissue paper, draw and cut out sea life, such as fish and coral,
using the dark-colored construction paper. Glue your sea life
onto the tissue paper. You can also use decorative paper
punches to create small starfish and bubbles. You may
also cover your frame with a printed tissue paper or color
it.

7. When you're done, flip over your Paper Aquarium and
hang it in the window so that when the light shines through,
your fish will come to life!
