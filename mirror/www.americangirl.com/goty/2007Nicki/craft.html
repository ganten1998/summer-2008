SUPPLIES

You will need:
  • key chain or travel tag with open/close flap for identification card
  • decorative adhesive backed paper (or decorative paper and clear drying glue stick)
  • pen or sharp-tip marker
  • scissors
  • ribbon
  • stickers

DIRECTIONS

1. Open the flap to remove the blank identification card. Trace this shape onto the back of the adhesive paper. Next trace the shape of the flap onto the back of the adhesive paper.
2. Cut out both shapes and peel off the backing.
3. Stick the identification card shape on the inside of the tag. Then stick the flap shape onto the outside of the flap.
4. If you plan to use your reminder tag as a key chain, skip to step 5. If not, remove the key chain ring. Fold a piece ribbon in half so that you are holding the two ends together in your hand. String both ends through the opening where the key chain ring was, but don't pull them all the way through so that the ribbon creates a loop. String the ends through the loop and pull to create a knot.
5. Decorate your reminder tag with stickers. Don't forget to include a special message inside.
