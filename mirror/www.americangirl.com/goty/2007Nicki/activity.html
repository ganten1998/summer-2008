Nicki Loses Track

"Mom, Becca wants to know if I can go with her to the movies after school on Friday, since we get out at noon," said Nicki. "Can I?"

"I thought you were going to Anderson's Pet Store to replace the leash that Sprocket chewed up," said Nicki's mom.

Nicki sighed. "Oh, yeah. I forgot."

"You've been doing that a lot lately. Are you sure you don't have too many things going on?"

Nicki thought about it. She had left her math book at school on Monday and her lunch on the bus on Tuesday-but those were just accidents. Then again, life had become pretty busy lately. She used to think that helping out with chores on the ranch took up a lot of time-but that was before she started training Sprocket to become a service dog, helping out on the Gala committee at school, and tutoring her younger brother. Now chores seemed a breeze compared to everything else! But it didn't matter. She'd taken on these responsibilities and people were counting on her.

Pushing away a trickle of panic, she focused on the issue at hand. "What if I pick up the leash on the way home from the movies? It's only a few blocks away. Besides, I haven't seen Becca in weeks-I don't want to let her down!"

"You know Becca will understand if you can't make it. But if you really want to go, and you promise to pick up the leash, I don't see why you can't. I'll even ask your dad if he'll walk Sprocket so that you only have to help with dinner when you get home."

"Thanks, Mom! Becca is going to be so excited!"

* * * *

"It's been so great to spend the whole afternoon together," sighed Nicki to Becca. "No chores to do, no dogs to train, and no brother to explain math problems to!"

"I know. It feels like I haven't seen you outside of school in ages!" Becca replied. "But you know what would make the afternoon even more perfect?" "What?"

"Ice cream!"

"Mmm! Let's go to the diner and split a banana split!" giggled Nicki.

"You read my mind," warbled Becca.

"Alright!"

Later, as the girls scraped the last bit of hot fudge from their dish, Nicki caught a glimpse of the clock behind the diner's counter. "It's 4:45? Oh, no! I told my mom I'd be home in time to help make dinner. I better run."

* * * *

"Just in time," said Nicki's dad, who was peeling potatoes over the sink. "Would you mind grabbing the celery out of the fridge and giving it a good wash?"

"No problem."

As Nicki put down her book bag and washed her hands, her mom-pregnant with twins-waddled into the room.

"How was the movie?"

"Honey, you're supposed to be resting-doctor's orders!" Nicki's dad frowned as his wife eased into a kitchen chair.

"I'm just going to sit here and watch you two cook," she replied, giving him a reassuring wink. "So, Nicki, how was it?"

"It was great-and really funny!"

"Good. And what color leash did you get for Sprocket?"

Nicki cringed as soon as she heard the word "leash." With all the fun she and Becca were having, the leash completely slipped her mind.

"Uh ... well, you see . . ."

"You forgot the leash." Her mom's voice was quiet, but Nicki could sense her disappointment.

"Yes. I'm sorry, I guess you were right-I have been pretty forgetful lately."

"Well, you do have a lot going on," added her dad. "So I figured I could help pick up the slack, just this once..." He reached into the broom closet and held up a new leather leash.

"Hey, a new leash! But how did you know I'd forget?"

"Actually, your mom figured it might happen...and even if it didn't, having an extra leash on hand for Sprocket, The Chewing Wonder, couldn't hurt."

Nicki felt a wash of relief so strong it almost brought tears to her eyes.

"It's not O.K. to be forgetful, but we know it happens-especially when you have a lot going on," added Nicki's mom. "So to help you remember your responsibilities, I made you this reminder tag. You can fasten it to your backpack, use it as a keychain, or wear it as a necklace. There's even a spot for special messages inside."

"Wow, thanks, both of you! I promise to use it. That reminds me, I haven't even checked on Sprocket since I came home. I'll go do that now."

"That's a great idea," said Nicki's dad. "As soon as you wash that celery."

"Guess I could put my reminder tag to use right now!" groaned Nicki.

But her parents only looked at each other and laughed.
