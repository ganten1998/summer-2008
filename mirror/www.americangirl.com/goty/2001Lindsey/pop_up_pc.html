PC - Windows 95/98 or 2000

Internet Explorer Users

  1. Click on the size you want your image (800 x 600, 1024 x 768, or 1280 x 1024) and when the image finishes loading in the browser window, right-click on the image.

  2. From the pop-up menu that appears, select "Set as Wallpaper."

  3. If you wish to change the appearance of your wallpaper, right-click on your desktop, select "Properties," and then select the "Background" tab. Under "Display" or "Picture Display," choose among center, tile, or stretch. Click "Apply" to view your choice, and click "OK" to exit Properties.


Netscape and Firefox Users

  1. Click on the size you want your image (800 x 600, 1024 x 768, or 1280 x 1024) and when the image finishes loading in the browser window, right-click on the image.

  2. From the pop-up menu that appears, select "Save Image As," and save the .jpg file in the Windows directory. Be sure to remember where you saved the file and what the file is named.

  3. Right-click on your desktop, select Properties, and then select the "Background" tab.

  4. Click the Browse button and find the file you saved. Select it and click "Open."

  5. Select the file, click "Apply" to view your choice, and click "OK" to exit Properties.

  6. If you wish to change the appearance of your wallpaper, right-click on your desktop, select Properties, and then select the "Background" tab. Under "Picture Display," choose among center, tile, or stretch. Click "Apply" to view your choice, and click "OK" to exit Properties.
