Parfleche
 

 

 
     

A parfleche was a rawhide pouch used to store important items such as clothing, hides, roots, dried meat, tools, and weaving materials. A parfleche was made by soaking buffalo, deer, or elk hide in water, sewing it together, and then decorating it. The parfleche was very stiff when new, but it became more flexible with use.
