An old woman not much taller than Addy appeared before Addy and Momma. "Miss Caroline? Can you help us?" Addy asked. Mistaking Addy for a boy from the Confederate camp, the woman turned them away. But Addy spoke up. "Me and my momma running away to freedom," she said, pulling off her hat to reveal her braids. "Can you help us?"

Momma and Addy were grateful that the hard part of their journey was now over. But there were many more dangerous miles to travel to freedom. Read Meet Addy and the rest of the Addy stories in the American Girls Collection to follow the rest of their journey--to freedom and to reuniting their broken family.
