The Church Fair

The church fair was an exciting place! The park was filled with wonderful sights and delicious smells. Almost everything sold at the fair was the handiwork of men, women, and children like Poppa, Momma, and Addy. People also donated their belongings--even precious items like wedding rings and pets--to be sold in booths. All of the money collected from the fair was used to help the victims of the war.
