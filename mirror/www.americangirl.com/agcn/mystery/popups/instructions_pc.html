Instructions: Windows 95/98 or 2000

Internet Explorer Users

  1. When the image finishes loading in the browser window, right-click on the image.
  2. From the pop-up menu that appears, select "Set as Wallpaper."
  3. If you wish to change the appearance of your wallpaper, right-click on your desktop, select "Properties," and then select the "Background" tab. Under "Display" or "Picture Display," choose among center, tile, or stretch. Click "Apply" to view your choice, and click "OK" to exit Properties.
