When you draw and paint with Samantha's Sketchbook, you will be using a Shockwave "cookie." This cookie is a small file that is saved on your computer's hard drive. It contains no personal information, like your name or e-mail address, but "remembers" that your computer has visited this feature before. So if you leave the site and then return, cookies will save the last nine sketches you created so that you can go back and see them and even work on them again. Basically, it makes returning to the feature more fun!
