Kit's Backyard
 

 
     

Painted metal patio chairs were a popular style throughout the 1920s and 1930s. They were featured in garden catalogues and magazines and furnished the backyards of families like Kit's during the Depression. The scrolled arms were fancy enough to suit Mother, and they were sturdy enough to last many years.
