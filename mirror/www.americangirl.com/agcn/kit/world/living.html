The Kittredge Living Room
 
     

The Kittredge living room was a very popular area of the house. Most evenings, boarders and family members gathered together to chat and listen to the radio. Before Kit and her brother were born, her parents may have used the room only for formal occasions. However, once they started their family and later took in boarders, the living room became a favorite gathering spot.
