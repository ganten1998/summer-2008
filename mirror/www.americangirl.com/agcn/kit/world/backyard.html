Kit's Backyard
 

 

 
     

Kit's mother's pride and joy were the gardens surrounding the Kittredge home. Before the Depression, she hosted garden club meetings on the terrace, entertaining guests amid the potted palms and fragrant phlox. She served dainty tea sandwiches and delicious desserts on fine china and starched table linens. The backyard was also the setting for Kit's Penny-Pincher Party---an idea inspired by lively Aunt Millie's money-saving ways.
