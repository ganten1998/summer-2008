Summer 2008 · An American Girl Archive

Welcome.

You're looking at a faithful, offline snapshot of americangirl.com as it existed around July 11, 2008 — the Nicki Fleming Girl-of-the-Year era, the Kit Kittredge film, the original eight historical characters before any retirements.

What plays

  • 112 Flash games and animations via Ruffle (with one-click Flash Player fallback)
  • 30 Shockwave Director games via bundled Wine + projector
  • Every preserved e-card, end-to-end customize → Mail.app send
  • Character sites, GotY contestant pages, movie subsite, travel, magazine activities, Coconut & Licorice

What's gone

  • The online shop — the e-commerce backend can't function offline
  • American Girl Magazine e-cards (agm category) — never crawled by any archive
  • The "Send for real" backend — replaced by a Mail.app handoff
  • A few games may stumble mid-play on Ruffle's ActionScript gaps; Flash Player fallback button is always one click away
